💛 WONDERLAB AR · OUR STORY

Museum-sized wonder, ready for any room.

We wanted every child's crayon drawing to swim in real 3D — without expensive scanners, tickets, or app downloads.

If you have ever watched a child color a fish at an interactive science museum and see it swim onto a giant glowing wall, you know that look of pure magic — the running up to the screen, pointing, and shouting "That one is mine!"

Traditionally, installations like that required expensive flatbed hardware scanners, dedicated servers, and big venue budgets. At WonderLab AR, we asked a simple question: what if any living-room TV, classroom projector, or birthday party wall could do the exact same thing — using only a sheet of paper and a regular phone browser?

Combining 3D artistry, custom-rigged sea creatures, 4-corner computer vision unwarping, and Live Smartphone Web AR, we built a reef where every child's drawing pops off the page in 3D right above their table — and then splashes into a shared ocean with all their friends' creations.
